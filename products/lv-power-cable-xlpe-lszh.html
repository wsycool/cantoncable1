Product Overview

Guangzhou Cable's CU/XLPE/LSZH power cable is a 0.6/1 kV low-voltage cable with a plain copper conductor, cross-linked polyethylene (XLPE) insulation and a low-smoke, zero-halogen (LSZH) thermoplastic sheath. It is designed for power distribution in locations where fire safety is critical — when the cable burns, it emits minimal smoke and no corrosive halogen gases, protecting both people and electronic equipment.

Manufactured under the TWINRHOMBI brand since 1956, this cable is produced with LME-registered copper and is type-tested by TÜV Rheinland for XLPE/PVC and LSZH constructions. Standard production lead time is 15–25 days, with free 1–2 m samples available for evaluation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the difference between CU/XLPE/LSZH and CU/XLPE/PVC cable?

Both share the same copper conductor and XLPE insulation. The LSZH version uses a low-smoke, zero-halogen sheath that emits no corrosive gases and far less smoke when burning — required in public buildings, tunnels and data centers. PVC sheaths are cheaper but release dense smoke and hydrochloric acid.
