Product Overview

The H05Z/H07Z series matches the H05V/H07V range in conductor types and sizes but uses a low-smoke, zero-halogen (LSZH) insulation. In a fire, ordinary PVC wires emit dense black smoke and corrosive hydrogen chloride that obscures escape routes and destroys electronics; LSZH wires emit minimal smoke and no halogen acids — increasingly mandated by building codes for public spaces.

Our H05Z/H07Z range is certified by TÜV Rheinland to BS EN 50525-3-31, available in solid (U), stranded (R) and flexible (K) conductors from 1.5 mm² to 95 mm².
