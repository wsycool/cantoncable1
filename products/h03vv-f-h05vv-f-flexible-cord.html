H03VV-F / H05VV-F PVC Flexible Cord

Multi-core PVC flexible cords to BS EN 50525-2-11 — the standard connection cable for household appliances, office equipment and light portable tools.

BS EN 50525-2-11IEC 60227H03VV-FH05VV-FOEM

Product Overview

H03VV-F (300/300 V) and H05VV-F (300/500 V) are the classic light PVC flexible cords used in appliances, lamps, office machines and portable equipment. Our cords use class 5 flexible copper conductors and high-grade PVC insulation and sheath, in 2, 3, 4 and 5 core versions from 0.5/0.75 mm² up to 4 mm².

The full range is produced under TÜV Rheinland-certified quality management and is available for OEM appliance manufacturers with custom colors, prints and lengths.
