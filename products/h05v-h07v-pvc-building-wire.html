Product Overview

The H05V/H07V series is the most widely used building wire family in the world. Our range covers H05V-U and H07V-U (solid conductor), H07V-R (stranded conductor, class 2) and H07V-K (flexible conductor, class 5) — in sizes from 0.5 mm² to 95 mm², with 300/500 V (H05) and 450/750 V (H07) ratings.

All constructions are certified by TÜV Rheinland to BS EN 50525-2-31, the harmonized European standard for single-core PVC wires. Conductor material is 99.99% electrolytic copper from LME-registered suppliers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the difference between H07V-R and H07V-K?

H07V-R uses a class 2 stranded conductor (fewer, thicker strands) for fixed installation; H07V-K uses a class 5 flexible conductor (many fine strands), making it easier to route through bends in panels and conduits.
