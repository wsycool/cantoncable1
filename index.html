About Us

Established in 1956, Guangzhou Cable Co., Ltd. is a wholly-owned enterprise under the Guangzhou State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission (SASAC). Located in Nansha District at the heart of the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area.

As a subsidiary of Guangzhou Industrial Investment Holdings Group (GIIHG) — a Fortune Global 500 enterprise — we specialize in the R&D, production, and sales of the "TWINRHOMBI" brand electric wires and cables.

Ranked among the Top 30 most competitive enterprises in China's cable industry, we provide comprehensive one-stop power transmission and distribution solutions.
